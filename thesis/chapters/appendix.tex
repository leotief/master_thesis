\section{The Characteristic Function as an Area}
\label{app:cf-area}

The characteristic function is used to compute $\E_{p(\xm \mid \xo)} \left[ \exp(isz) \right]$.
The integrand depends on $\xm$ only through $z = w^T x$, so we can take the expectation over the distribution $p(z \mid \xo)$ of $z$ instead.
With Euler's formula $e^{isz} = \cos(sz) + i \sin(sz)$, the imaginary part of $\exp(isz)$ is the wave $\sin(sz)$:
\[
  \mathrm{Im}\, \phi_z(s) = \E_{p(z \mid \xo)} \left[ \sin(sz) \right] = \int \sin(sz)\, p(z \mid \xo) \, \mathrm{d}z.
\]
This is the area under the wave times $p(z \mid \xo)$, where parts below zero count negatively.
So for every frequency $s$, the characteristic function gives the signed area under the wave times $p(z \mid \xo)$.
